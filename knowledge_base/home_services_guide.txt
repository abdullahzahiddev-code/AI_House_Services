PLUMBING SERVICES
-----------------
A plumber is a professional who installs, repairs, and maintains pipes, fixtures, and other plumbing equipment
used for water distribution and waste-water disposal in residential buildings.

Common plumbing problems in Pakistani homes:
- Kitchen sink leaking from underneath the cabinet
- Bathroom tap dripping or not shutting off completely
- Toilet flushing issues or running water continuously
- Blocked or clogged drains in kitchen/bathroom
- Water pressure low throughout the house
- Pipe burst or cracked due to pressure or age
- Sewage smell coming from drains
- Water heater (geyser) leaking from top or bottom
- Main water supply valve leaking
- Water meter area leakage

Signs you need a plumber:
- Water pooling under the sink
- Gurgling sounds from drains
- Water stains on walls or ceilings
- Slow-draining sinks or tubs
- Unusually high water bills (possible hidden leak)
- Bad odor from drains or pipes

Required plumber skills: pipe repair, leak detection, drain cleaning, fixture installation,
water heater repair, pipe fitting, pressure testing, sewer line repair

Typical charges in Rawalpindi/Islamabad: Rs. 500 - 10,000 depending on job complexity

ELECTRICAL SERVICES
-------------------
An electrician is a tradesperson specializing in electrical wiring of buildings,
transmission lines, stationary machines, and related equipment.

Common electrical problems in Pakistani homes:
- Power tripping frequently (MCB/breaker keeps flipping)
- Electrical switch not working or making sparking sound
- Socket/outlet not providing power
- Fan running slow or making noise
- Light bulb flickering or not working
- Wiring problem — burning smell from switchboard
- Power load issues during peak hours
- UPS or inverter wiring and connection
- Generator connection and automatic changeover
- New point installation (fan, AC, water pump)

DANGER WARNING for electrical issues:
If you see sparks, smell burning, or see smoke from electrical components,
immediately turn off the main circuit breaker and do NOT touch any wires.
Call a qualified electrician immediately.

Required electrician skills: wiring, switch repair, breaker installation, fan installation,
load calculation, AC wiring, generator connection, UPS installation

Typical charges in Rawalpindi/Islamabad: Rs. 500 - 8,000 depending on job

AC TECHNICIAN / HVAC SERVICES
------------------------------
An AC technician specializes in the installation, maintenance, and repair of air conditioning
and heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems.

Common AC problems in Pakistani homes:
- AC running but not cooling the room
- AC making loud noise or vibration
- AC remote not working
- Gas/refrigerant leaking (noticeable if cooling suddenly reduces)
- AC compressor not starting
- Water dripping from indoor unit
- Ice forming on coils
- AC suddenly turning off
- Outdoor unit fan not spinning
- AC giving warm air instead of cold

Required AC technician skills: refrigerant handling, compressor repair, filter cleaning,
gas charging, PCB troubleshooting, electrical diagnosis, split unit installation

Typical charges in Rawalpindi/Islamabad: Rs. 1,000 - 15,000 depending on repair type

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: How long does a typical plumbing repair take?
A: Minor repairs (tap replacement, drain cleaning) take 30-60 minutes.
   Major work (pipe replacement, geyser installation) takes 2-4 hours.

Q: Is it safe to use appliances with flickering lights?
A: No. Flickering lights can indicate loose wiring or overloaded circuits.
   Turn off the breaker and call an electrician immediately.

Q: How often should I service my AC?
A: AC should be serviced at least once per year, ideally before summer.
   Filter cleaning should be done every 2-3 months.

Q: What should I do if I smell gas at home?
A: IMMEDIATELY open windows and doors. Do NOT use any electrical switches.
   Evacuate the house and call SNGPL at 1199 or Rescue at 1122.

Q: Can I fix a leaking pipe myself?
A: Only very minor drips at accessible fittings should be attempted by non-professionals.
   For any pipe inside walls or major leaks, always call a qualified plumber.
